ABOUT ME

I am based in Port Moresby working for a fairly new locally owned Internet Service Provider (ISP) - Next Town Technology Ltd and have been gethering experience for about 5+ years as a Core Network & Application Officer.

Core Network Engineer - I plan and implement both company and clients network infrastructure as well as manage monitor and provide IT support to resolve network issues. I also plan and implement Gigabit Passive Optical Network (GPON), doing main fiber optics hauling, splicing and splitter installations.

Application Officer - I design and build full stack websites, research alternate update to date solutions, research and develop products and solutions, design company systems and processes, and record, manage and maintain company data.

Manage Operations - compiling and reporting of daily tasks, assigning and supervising officers tasks, managing and maintaining clients information, and occasionally doing sales and marketing.

BIO:

Born and raised in the Capital of Papua New Guinea, I am the eldest of 5 brothers with mixed parentage from East Sepik and New Ireland provinces. My hobbies include researching and testing free open source solutions, building my homelab, and playing chess or any fun strategic games.
